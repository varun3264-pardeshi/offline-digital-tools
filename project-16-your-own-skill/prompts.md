### Prompt 1: Standardized Agent Skill Specification (`SKILL.md`)

### Prompt 2: Examiner Feedback & Grading Rubric
> "Program the agent to evaluate candidate answers with authentic professor-level scrutiny: acknowledging partially correct elements while highlighting missing foundational principles (e.g. 'Partly. Friction lowers real engines, but what about an ideal one?'). At the conclusion of 5 questions, output a score out of 5 and pinpoint the exact syllabus page requiring revision."

### Prompt 3: Verification Across 3 Distinct Subjects
> "Verify the skill's performance across 3 distinct subject domains:
> 1. Thermodynamics Notes (Carnot efficiency, Zeroth & Second laws)
> 2. Data Structures Notes (AVL trees, binary search, hash collisions)
> 3. Machine Learning Notes (Attention mechanism, vanishing gradients, Adam optimizer)."

### Prompt 4: Before vs After Demonstration (Without Skill vs With Skill)
