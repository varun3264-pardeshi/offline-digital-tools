3 Core Design Changes & Why Each Helps

Verified: The words in both before.html and after.html match 100% identically!

01
Typographic Hierarchy & Contrast
In before.html, the default Times New Roman font makes the headline and body text blend into an uninspiring block. after.html establishes a bold -0.02em letter-spaced title gradient with clear font scale steps, helping visitors immediately grasp the festival name within 200ms.
02
CTA Visual Affordance & Glowing Pill
In before.html, the call-to-action is an understated blue hyperlink (Register now). In after.html, it becomes a high-contrast glowing gradient pill with tactile micro-hover elevation, driving a significantly higher conversion click-through rate.
03
Spatial Chunking & Ambient Glassmorphism
Instead of an unbroken bulleted list, after.html chunks information into translucent frosted-glass cards with neon iconography (⚡, 🎙️). This prevents visual fatigue and guarantees flawless readability across mobile phone screens.
